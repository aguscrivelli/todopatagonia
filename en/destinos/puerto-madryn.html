Puerto Madryn: the base for Valdés Peninsula

A city on Golfo Nuevo, with whales in sight in season.

Why Visit

🐋
Whales from shore

In season, southern right whales come so close to some beaches in the gulf that you can see them without a boat.

🧭
Base for Valdés Peninsula

Most tours to the peninsula leave from the city.

🏛️
Welsh history

Welsh settlers landed on this coast in 1865 and their legacy is still present in the region.

What to Do

Waterfront and beaches

Several kilometers of waterfront for walking or cycling along the gulf.

Whale watching from shore

North of the city there are beaches where, in season, whales can be seen very close to shore.

Punta Loma

A nearby reserve with a sea lion colony and coastal viewpoints.

Diving and snorkeling

Golfo Nuevo's waters are a well-known diving destination, with trips for beginners and experienced divers.

Ecocentro

An interpretation center about the Patagonian sea, on a headland along the coast.

Day trips

From the city you can visit Valdés Peninsula, the Punta Tombo penguin colony in season and the Welsh towns of the Chubut valley.

How to Get There

By plane: Puerto Madryn has an airport (PMY), and many travelers fly into Trelew (REL), about an hour away by road.

By bus: long-distance services run from Buenos Aires and other cities.

Getting around: tours with transfers or a rental car, very handy for exploring the peninsula at your own pace.

Where to Stay

💰 Budget

Hostels with shared rooms and guest kitchens, plus campsites in season. They're the most affordable option and a good place to meet other travelers.

🏨 Mid-range

Inns, cabins and small hotels, often with breakfast included. Cabins with kitchens suit families and groups.

⭐ Luxury

Upscale hotels and lodges with landscape views, spas or tours included. Book them well in advance.

Practical Tips

🐋
When to go

For whales, roughly June to December. In summer, the city is also a beach destination.

🌬️
Changeable weather

Wind and sudden weather changes are part of the trip. Dress in layers and always carry a windbreaker.

📅
Book ahead

In high season, lodging and tours fill up. It's best to book as soon as you have your dates.

💵
Prices and payments

Prices change often: check current rates before you travel and carry some cash as well as cards.
