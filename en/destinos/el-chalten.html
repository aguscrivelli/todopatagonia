El Chaltén: hiking at the foot of Fitz Roy

Argentina's National Trekking Capital, with trails that start right from town.

Why Visit

⛰️
Fitz Roy and Cerro Torre

Two of the most impressive mountains in the Andes, a day's hike from town.

🥾
Trails from town

You don't need a car or a tour: many trails start in the streets of El Chaltén.

🍂
Lenga forests

In autumn the forests turn red and yellow and the landscape changes completely.

What to Do

Laguna de los Tres

The classic hike to Fitz Roy: a long day with a very steep final section. Start early and pick a clear day.

Laguna Torre

A full day without big climbs, to a lake with the glacier and Cerro Torre behind it.

Mirador de los Cóndores

A short walk with views over the town and valley. Ideal for your arrival day or sunset.

Loma del Pliegue Tumbado

A steady climb to a viewpoint with Fitz Roy, Cerro Torre and Lake Viedma on the same horizon.

Lago del Desierto

North of town, a day trip among forests, waterfalls and quiet lakes.

How to Get There

The nearest airport is El Calafate (FTE), about 220 km away. There are daily buses to El Chaltén, a trip of around three hours.

By car: via Route 40 and provincial route 23, with steppe scenery and views of Fitz Roy as you approach.

In town: everything is walkable. Stop at the national park visitor center at the entrance for trail information.

Where to Stay

💰 Budget

Hostels with shared rooms and guest kitchens, plus campsites in season. They're the most affordable option and a good place to meet other travelers.

🏨 Mid-range

Inns, cabins and small hotels, often with breakfast included. Cabins with kitchens suit families and groups.

⭐ Luxury

Upscale hotels and lodges with landscape views, spas or tours included. Book them well in advance.

Practical Tips

☀️
Best time

The trekking season runs from October to April. In winter many lodgings and shops close.

🌬️
Changeable weather

Wind and sudden weather changes are part of the trip. Dress in layers and always carry a windbreaker.

📅
Book ahead

In high season, lodging and tours fill up. It's best to book as soon as you have your dates.

💵
Prices and payments

Prices change often: check current rates before you travel and carry some cash as well as cards.

Leave spare days: Fitz Roy often hides in the clouds, and the day it clears is your Laguna de los Tres day.
