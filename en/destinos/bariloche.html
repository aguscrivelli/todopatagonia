Bariloche: lakes, mountains and forests

On the shores of Lake Nahuel Huapi, a year-round destination.

Why Visit

🏞️
Nahuel Huapi National Park

Lakes, forests and mountains inside Argentina's oldest national park.

⛷️
Snow in winter

Cerro Catedral is one of South America's best-known ski resorts.

🍫
Food

Chocolate, craft beer and mountain dishes with trout and venison.

What to Do

Circuito Chico

The classic drive along Nahuel Huapi to the Llao Llao Peninsula, by car, bike or tour.

Cerro Campanario

A chairlift takes you up in minutes to one of the area's best panoramic views.

Victoria Island and Arrayanes Forest

A boat trip from Puerto Pañuelo to the island and the cinnamon-barked arrayán forest.

Cerro Catedral

Skiing in winter and, in summer, lifts and trails such as the one to Refugio Frey.

Mount Tronador

A full-day trip to the area's highest mountain and the Ventisquero Negro glacier.

How to Get There

By plane: Bariloche International Airport (BRC) has direct flights from Buenos Aires, about two hours, and from other cities.

By bus: long-distance services run from Buenos Aires and other cities; the trip from the capital takes about a day.

From Chile: overland via the Cardenal Samoré Pass, which links the area with Osorno and Puerto Montt.

Where to Stay

💰 Budget

Hostels with shared rooms and guest kitchens, plus campsites in season. They're the most affordable option and a good place to meet other travelers.

🏨 Mid-range

Inns, cabins and small hotels, often with breakfast included. Cabins with kitchens suit families and groups.

⭐ Luxury

Upscale hotels and lodges with landscape views, spas or tours included. Book them well in advance.

Practical Tips

☀️
Best time

Summer for lakes and hiking; winter for snow. January and July are the busiest months.

🌬️
Changeable weather

Wind and sudden weather changes are part of the trip. Dress in layers and always carry a windbreaker.

📅
Book ahead

In high season, lodging and tours fill up. It's best to book as soon as you have your dates.

💵
Prices and payments

Prices change often: check current rates before you travel and carry some cash as well as cards.
