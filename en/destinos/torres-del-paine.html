Torres del Paine: Chilean Patagonia's great park

Granite towers, turquoise lakes and glaciers in southern Chile.

Why Visit

🗻
The Paine massif

The Towers and the Horns of Paine form one of the world's most famous mountain landscapes.

🥾
Trekking

The W and O circuits are among South America's most sought-after treks.

🦙
Wildlife

Guanacos, rheas, condors and one of the best chances to see wild pumas.

What to Do

Base of the Towers viewpoint

A long, demanding hike ending in front of the three granite towers. It can be done as a day hike.

W Trek

Several days through the park's best-known valleys. Requires confirmed bookings at refugios or campsites.

Lake Grey

A lake with icebergs and the front of Grey Glacier, reached by boat trips in season.

Viewpoints by car

Salto Grande, the Cuernos viewpoint and Lake Pehoé can be visited on short walks from the road.

Puma tracking

Specialized tours with trackers greatly improve the odds of seeing one.

How to Get There

From Chile: fly to Punta Arenas (PUQ) and take a bus to Puerto Natales, the base town, and from there to the park.

From Argentina: most travelers combine it with El Calafate, crossing overland to Puerto Natales.

To the park: there are buses from Puerto Natales, day tours and rental cars. There's an entrance fee, best arranged before your trip.

Where to Stay

💰 Budget

Campsites inside the park, booked in advance, and guesthouses in Puerto Natales.

🏨 Mid-range

Refugios on the trek and hotels in Puerto Natales, the usual choice for day trips.

⭐ Luxury

Hotels and lodges inside the park, many with full board and excursions included.

Practical Tips

🛂
Border and bookings

If you come from Argentina, carry valid documents. You need confirmed bookings to sleep inside the park.

🌬️
Changeable weather

Wind and sudden weather changes are part of the trip. Dress in layers and always carry a windbreaker.

📅
Book ahead

In high season, lodging and tours fill up. It's best to book as soon as you have your dates.

💵
Prices and payments

Prices change often: check current rates before you travel and carry some cash as well as cards.

Even with a single day, Torres del Paine is worth the trip. If you can, stay longer and explore it on foot.
