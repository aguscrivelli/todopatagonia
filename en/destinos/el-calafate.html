El Calafate: glaciers and lakes in Patagonia

The gateway to Perito Moreno Glacier, on the shores of Lake Argentino.

Why Visit

🧊
Perito Moreno

One of the most accessible glaciers in the world: from the walkways you see it face to face and hear the ice break off.

🌍
World Heritage Site

Los Glaciares National Park is recognized by UNESCO for its ice fields and glaciers.

🚤
Lakes and icebergs

Boat trips on Lake Argentino reach other glaciers and sail among icebergs of incredible colors.

🥾
Base for El Chaltén

From El Calafate you can continue by road to El Chaltén, the trekking capital.

What to Do

Perito Moreno walkways

Several kilometers of trails facing the glacier, with balconies at different heights. Stay a while in one spot to watch the ice calve.

Walking on the ice

Crampon hikes on the glacier, always with licensed guides. Spots and age limits apply, and they sell out in high season.

Boat trips on Lake Argentino

Boat trips in front of Perito Moreno and longer excursions to other glaciers in the park, such as Upsala and Spegazzini.

Laguna Nimez Reserve

Steps from downtown, a trail through wetlands where you can see flamingos, black-necked swans and many other birds.

Glaciarium

An interpretation center dedicated to glaciers, ideal for understanding what you'll see in the park.

How to Get There

By plane: El Calafate International Airport (FTE) has direct flights from Buenos Aires, about 3 hours, and connections with other cities. It's about 20 km from town.

Overland: there are buses from Río Gallegos, from El Chaltén (about 220 km away) and from Puerto Natales, Chile.

To the glacier: Perito Moreno is about 80 km away on a paved road. Go on a tour, by scheduled bus or by car.

Where to Stay

💰 Budget

Hostels with shared rooms and guest kitchens, plus campsites in season. They're the most affordable option and a good place to meet other travelers.

🏨 Mid-range

Inns, cabins and small hotels, often with breakfast included. Cabins with kitchens suit families and groups.

⭐ Luxury

Upscale hotels and lodges with landscape views, spas or tours included. Book them well in advance.

Practical Tips

☀️
Best time

From October to April all tours run and days are long. The park is open all year.

🌬️
Changeable weather

Wind and sudden weather changes are part of the trip. Dress in layers and always carry a windbreaker.

📅
Book ahead

In high season, lodging and tours fill up. It's best to book as soon as you have your dates.

💵
Prices and payments

Prices change often: check current rates before you travel and carry some cash as well as cards.
