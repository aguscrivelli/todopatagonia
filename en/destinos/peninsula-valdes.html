Valdés Peninsula: whales and marine wildlife

One of the best places in the world to see whales from shore and at sea.

Why Visit

🐋
Southern right whale

Every year they arrive in the gulfs to breed; the season runs roughly from June to December.

🦭
Wildlife all year

Sea lions and elephant seals live on the peninsula's shores much of the year.

🌍
World Heritage Site

The peninsula is recognized by UNESCO for the importance of its marine wildlife.

What to Do

Whale-watching boat trips

In season, boat trips leave from Puerto Pirámides, the only town on the peninsula.

Whales from shore

In parts of the gulf, such as the coast near Puerto Madryn, whales come very close to shore.

Driving around the peninsula

Wildlife viewpoints such as Punta Delgada, Caleta Valdés and Punta Norte, linked by gravel roads.

Penguins

In spring and summer, Magellanic penguins arrive on the Chubut coast; Punta Tombo, to the south, is one of the best-known colonies.

Puerto Madryn

The base town, with a waterfront, beaches and diving in Golfo Nuevo.

How to Get There

By plane: the most used airports are Trelew (REL) and Puerto Madryn (PMY). From Trelew it's about an hour by road to Puerto Madryn.

By bus: long-distance services reach Puerto Madryn from Buenos Aires and other cities.

On the peninsula: explore on a tour from Puerto Madryn or by car. Inner roads are gravel: drive carefully.

Where to Stay

💰 Budget

Hostels with shared rooms and guest kitchens, plus campsites in season. They're the most affordable option and a good place to meet other travelers.

🏨 Mid-range

Inns, cabins and small hotels, often with breakfast included. Cabins with kitchens suit families and groups.

⭐ Luxury

Upscale hotels and lodges with landscape views, spas or tours included. Book them well in advance.

Practical Tips

🐋
When to go

For whales, roughly June to December. Animal cycles vary a little each year: check before you travel.

🌬️
Changeable weather

Wind and sudden weather changes are part of the trip. Dress in layers and always carry a windbreaker.

📅
Book ahead

In high season, lodging and tours fill up. It's best to book as soon as you have your dates.

💵
Prices and payments

Prices change often: check current rates before you travel and carry some cash as well as cards.
