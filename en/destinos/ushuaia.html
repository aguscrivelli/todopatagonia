Ushuaia: the city at the end of the world

Between the mountains and the Beagle Channel, at Argentina's southern tip.

Why Visit

🌊
The Beagle Channel

Boat trips among islands with sea lions, birds and the Les Eclaireurs lighthouse.

🐧
Penguins

In season you can visit the Martillo Island penguin colony, with trips from the city.

🌲
Tierra del Fuego National Park

Forests, lakes and bays a few kilometers away, with trails for every level.

What to Do

Beagle Channel boat trip

Ushuaia's classic outing: islands with marine wildlife and views of the city from the water.

Tierra del Fuego National Park

Coastal and forest trails, lakes and Lapataia Bay. Go on your own or on a tour.

End of the World Train

A historic ride that enters the national park. Great with kids.

Laguna Esmeralda

A half-day hike through forest and peat bog to a lake of intense green.

Martial Glacier

A trail climbing from the edge of the city, with views of the channel.

Maritime and Prison Museum

Housed in Ushuaia's old prison, it tells the story of the city and southern navigation.

How to Get There

By plane: Malvinas Argentinas International Airport (USH) has direct flights from Buenos Aires, about three and a half hours, and from other cities.

Overland: from the mainland you cross the Strait of Magellan by ferry and pass through Chilean territory. It's a long trip that requires valid documents.

Around town: taxis, private cars and tours with transfers. The national park is a few kilometers away.

Where to Stay

💰 Budget

Hostels with shared rooms and guest kitchens, plus campsites in season. They're the most affordable option and a good place to meet other travelers.

🏨 Mid-range

Inns, cabins and small hotels, often with breakfast included. Cabins with kitchens suit families and groups.

⭐ Luxury

Upscale hotels and lodges with landscape views, spas or tours included. Book them well in advance.

Practical Tips

☀️
Best time

In summer the days are very long and there are more tours. In winter, Ushuaia is a snow destination.

🌬️
Changeable weather

Wind and sudden weather changes are part of the trip. Dress in layers and always carry a windbreaker.

📅
Book ahead

In high season, lodging and tours fill up. It's best to book as soon as you have your dates.

💵
Prices and payments

Prices change often: check current rates before you travel and carry some cash as well as cards.

Ushuaia combines sea, mountains and forest within a few kilometers. Three or four days cover the essentials.
