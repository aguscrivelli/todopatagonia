Ushuaia: what to do at the end of the world

Ushuaia blends sea, mountains and forest in a city on the shores of the Beagle Channel. Here are the must-do outings and how to plan your days at the end of the world.

📅 November 27, 2026 📍 Ushuaia ⏱️ 6 min read

A city between the sea and the mountains

Ushuaia, the capital of Tierra del Fuego, sits on the shore of the Beagle Channel with mountain ranges behind it. It's one of the southernmost cities in the world and the gateway to landscapes you won't find anywhere else: lenga and ñire forests, peat bogs, hanging glaciers and islets full of marine wildlife. In just a few days you can combine boat trips, hikes and a bit of history.

Tierra del Fuego National Park

A few kilometers west of town along National Route 3 lies a national park that combines a sea coast with Andean forest. The drive ends at Lapataia Bay, where a sign marks the end of Route 3, one of the most photographed spots in the area.

The park has trails for every level:

  • Short walks: Mirador Lapataia, Paseo de la Isla, Senda del Turbal and Laguna Negra take less than an hour and are open year-round.
  • Coastal Trail: about 8 km along the Beagle Channel, through forest and pebble beaches, of moderate difficulty.
  • Hito XXIV: follows Lake Acigami (formerly Lake Roca) to the border with Chile.
  • Cerro Guanaco: a demanding climb with a big elevation gain and wide views; only for those with mountain experience.

The longer trails open during the warm season, roughly November to April. Inside the park speeds are low, and you should watch for foxes, guanacos and other animals on the road.

Beagle Channel boat trips

This is Ushuaia's classic outing. Boats leave from the port and visit Bird Island, with cormorants, gulls and other seabirds; Sea Lion Island, with colonies of sea lions on the rocks; and the Les Éclaireurs Lighthouse, the famous red-and-white lighthouse in the channel. Depending on the option, trips last half a day or a little longer.

Penguins on Martillo Island

Some boat trips and tours reach Martillo Island, home to a colony of Magellanic penguins, now slowly joined by a few gentoo penguins. The penguins arrive around October and stay through spring and summer, roughly until March. Outside that season there are no visits to the colony.

Martial Glacier

About 7 km from downtown, on the mountain overlooking the city, Martial Glacier is an ideal hike for your first day. The trail climbs through the forest from the base to a viewpoint over the channel and Ushuaia. In summer it's an accessible trek; in winter the area is covered in snow.

Laguna Esmeralda

This is one of the most beautiful hikes in the area. The trailhead is about 18 km from town on Route 3, and the trail covers around 9 km round trip through forest and peat bogs to a deep green lake at the foot of the mountains. Plan on about five hours in total. The ground is wet and muddy, so waterproof footwear is almost a must.

History: the old prison

Ushuaia grew around a prison built in the late 19th century. Today the old prison building is a museum that tells the story of the inmates, the city and navigation in the southern seas. It's a good plan for a rainy day, which are not rare in Ushuaia.

Beyond the city

If you have an extra day, Route 3 heading north crosses Garibaldi Pass and descends toward Lake Escondido and Lake Fagnano, surrounded by forest. In winter, the downhill and cross-country ski areas near the city turn Ushuaia into a snow destination.

Practical tips

  • Dress in layers: the weather changes quickly, and it can be cold and windy even in midsummer.
  • Bring waterproof footwear: peat bogs and trails are often wet.
  • Check current fees: Tierra del Fuego National Park charges an entrance fee; check rates and opening hours on the National Parks website.
  • Pick the season for what you want to see: penguins are around from spring to late summer; snow, in winter.
  • Leave room in your schedule: wind sometimes cancels boat trips, so keep a spare day.
  • Respect wildlife: don't feed the animals and keep your distance.
