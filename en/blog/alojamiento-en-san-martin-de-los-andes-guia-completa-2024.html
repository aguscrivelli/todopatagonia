Where to stay in San Martín de los Andes: complete guide

San Martín de los Andes has options for every budget, from inns and cabins to hostels and campsites. We help you choose the area and type of lodging that fits your trip.

📅 November 25, 2026 📍 San Martín de los Andes ⏱️ 5 min read

A mountain town on Lake Lácar

San Martín de los Andes, in Neuquén province, is a quiet mountain town of low houses and tree-lined streets at the head of Lake Lácar. It is the gateway to Lanín National Park and one end of the Seven Lakes Road. It works all year round: in summer for the lake and the trails, in winter for skiing at Cerro Chapelco.

Getting there

  • By plane: Chapelco airport is about 20 kilometers away, between San Martín and Junín de los Andes, with flights from Buenos Aires. Frequencies vary by season.
  • By car: from Bariloche, take the Seven Lakes Road, a drive of about 200 kilometers that deserves to be done slowly.
  • By bus: there are long-distance services from Buenos Aires, Neuquén and Bariloche.

Areas to stay

Town center

The most practical choice if you don't have a car. You're a few blocks from the waterfront, restaurants, shops and tour agencies. It's also the area with the widest range of inns, hotels and hostels.

Waterfront and lakeside

Ideal if you want views of Lake Lácar and sunsets over the water. It's usually a bit pricier than the center, but the location makes up for it.

Road to Cerro Chapelco

On the outskirts, surrounded by forest. There are many cabins and complexes with more space and peace. It's the natural choice in winter if you're going skiing, but you'll need a car or depend on shuttles.

Lake Lolog area

For total disconnection, with cabins and houses in natural settings a few kilometers from town.

Types of lodging

  • Inns and hotels: the town's classic option, often mountain-style, with a fireplace and breakfast included.
  • Cabins: very popular with families and groups. They have kitchens, which helps save on meals.
  • Apartment hotels and rentals: a good middle ground for longer stays.
  • Hostels: for backpackers and budget travelers, with shared kitchens and a friendly atmosphere.
  • Campsites: there are campsites in town and along the lakeshore, some open only in summer.

Seasons and prices

The busiest months are January, February and July, due to summer and winter holidays. Easter week and long weekends also fill up. During those periods prices rise and availability drops, so book well in advance. Spring and autumn are quieter, with better rates and beautiful scenery.

Practical tips

  • Book early for July: ski season concentrates a lot of demand in a few weeks.
  • Ask about heating: winter nights are very cold.
  • Consider a car: if you stay outside the center, it gives you a lot of freedom.
  • Choose flexible cancellation: mountain weather can change your plans.
  • Read recent reviews: they give a more accurate picture of the property today.
