What to Do in Patagonia?

🏔️

Trekking

From family-friendly walks to multi-day expeditions in the world's wildest parks.

🧊

Glaciers

Walk on ancient ice or sail among icebergs in El Calafate and Torres del Paine.

🐋

Wildlife Watching

Whales, orcas, penguins and pumas in their natural habitat. Patagonia is a wildlife sanctuary.

🎿

Ski & Snowboard

Patagonia's ski resorts: Cerro Catedral, Chapelco and Cerro Castor.

🚣

Kayak & Rafting

Crystal-clear rivers and impossibly colored lakes. Patagonia's water is another world.

📸

Photography

Breathtaking landscapes around every corner. An ideal destination for photography.
