Things to do in Patagonia

Glaciers, mountains, lakes and wildlife: these are the experiences that define a trip to the far south.

Last updated: November 2026

Things to do in Patagonia

🥾

Hiking & trekking

Patagonia has some of the world's most famous trails, with options for every level: from short walks to viewpoints to multi-day treks.

  • Laguna de los Tres and Laguna Torre, El Chaltén
  • W Trek, Torres del Paine
  • Refugio Frey, Bariloche
🗓 October to April 💪 Moderate

🧊

Glaciers

Seeing a glacier up close is one of the most powerful experiences of the trip: from walkways, on a boat in front of the ice walls or hiking on the glacier with guides.

  • Perito Moreno Glacier, El Calafate
  • Boat trips on Lake Argentino
  • Grey Glacier, Torres del Paine
🗓 All year; more activities from October to April 💪 Easy

🐋

Wildlife

Whales, penguins, sea lions, guanacos and condors. Patagonia is one of the best places in South America to see wildlife in the wild.

  • Valdés Peninsula, Chubut
  • Martillo Island, Beagle Channel
  • Torres del Paine, Chile
🗓 Depends on the species: whales, roughly June to December 💪 Easy

Boat trips & kayaking

Patagonia's lakes and channels can be explored by boat or kayak, surrounded by islands, forests and mountains.

  • Beagle Channel, Ushuaia
  • Victoria Island and Arrayanes Forest, Bariloche
  • Lake Argentino, El Calafate
🗓 October to April 💪 Easy

Skiing & snow

In winter, Patagonia's ski resorts welcome skiers from around the world, with slopes overlooking lakes and forests.

  • Cerro Catedral, Bariloche
  • Cerro Chapelco, San Martín de los Andes
  • Cerro Castor, Ushuaia
🗓 June to September, depending on snow 💪 Moderate

Scenic drives

Some of the most beautiful scenery is enjoyed from the road, stopping at viewpoints, lakes and small towns.

  • Seven Lakes Road, Neuquén
  • Circuito Chico, Bariloche
  • Route 40 between El Calafate and El Chaltén

When to go

Spring · September - November

Whale season at Valdés Peninsula and the arrival of penguins on the coast.

🎯 Wildlife · Boat trips & kayaking

Summer · December - February

Very long days and all trails and tours running. It's high season.

🎯 Hiking & trekking · Glaciers · Boat trips & kayaking

Autumn · March - May

Forests turn red and yellow and there are fewer visitors.

🎯 Hiking & trekking · Scenic drives

Winter · June - August

Snow in the Andes and ski season. Some trails and tours close.

🎯 Skiing & snow · Glaciers

💡 Planning tips

  • Dress in layers — A single day can bring sun, wind, rain and cold. A good windbreaker is essential.
  • Book ahead — In high season, the most popular tours sell out weeks in advance.
  • Leave spare days — The weather is in charge. An extra day can save a hike or a boat trip.
  • Follow park rules — Stay on the trails, don't feed wildlife and pack out your waste.

Patagonia is best enjoyed slowly. Choose a few bases, leave time for the weather and let it surprise you.
