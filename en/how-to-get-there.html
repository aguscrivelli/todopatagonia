Getting to Patagonia: Your Complete Travel Guide

Patagonia's remote beauty requires careful travel planning. Learn the best routes by air and land to reach Argentina and Chile's most spectacular destinations.

Last updated: November 2026

Reaching Patagonia from major hubs requires a combination of domestic flights and overland travel. Buenos Aires and Santiago serve as primary gateways, with most visitors flying to regional airports before exploring the region. Understanding your transport options ensures a smooth journey to this remote corner of South America.

Por destino

🏔️ Bariloche

✈️ Aeropuerto: Bariloche International Airport (BRC)

🇦🇷 Desde Buenos Aires: Direct flights available (2.5-3 hours) on Aerolíneas Argentinas, LATAM, and Flybondi. Buses depart Buenos Aires daily (20-24 hours) but are rarely recommended given the distance. Flying is the practical choice for most travelers.

🇨🇱 Desde Santiago: Flights to Bariloche operate daily (3.5-4 hours) through Santiago or direct connections. Overland via bus takes 20+ hours through the Andes.

🚌 Transporte local: Rent a car for maximum flexibility exploring Circuito Chico and surrounding lakes. City center is walkable, with local buses and remises (ride-hailing) available.

💡 Book flights 2-3 weeks in advance during high season (December-February) as Bariloche fills quickly with both domestic and international tourists.

🧊 El Calafate

✈️ Aeropuerto: Gobernador Gregores International Airport (FRC)

🇦🇷 Desde Buenos Aires: Flights daily (3-3.5 hours) with Aerolíneas Argentinas and LATAM. Some flights connect through Buenos Aires. Buses available (28+ hours) but flying is strongly recommended for this distance.

🇨🇱 Desde Santiago: Flights available via Buenos Aires connection or direct service on some dates (5-6 hours total). Bus options exist but require 24+ hours of travel.

🚌 Transporte local: Car rental essential for visiting Perito Moreno Glacier and El Calafate National Park. The town itself is compact and walkable.

💡 Arrive the day before your glacier tour to acclimate and ensure you don't miss your reserved time slot due to flight delays.

🌍 Ushuaia

✈️ Aeropuerto: Ushuaia International Airport (USH)

🇦🇷 Desde Buenos Aires: Flights require connection through El Calafate or Río Gallegos (5-6 hours total travel time). No direct flights available. Flying is the only practical option; buses don't operate this far south.

🇨🇱 Desde Santiago: Flights connect through Buenos Aires or El Calafate (8+ hours total). This is a lengthy journey best broken into multiple days.

🚌 Transporte local: Walking the compact city center is feasible. Hire a guide or book excursions for Tierra del Fuego National Park and Cape Horn tours. Local buses and remises available.

💡 Book your flight to Ushuaia well in advance as the southern route has limited daily flights and high demand, especially in summer months.

⛰️ El Chaltén

✈️ Aeropuerto: Gobernador Gregores International Airport (FRC) in El Calafate

🇦🇷 Desde Buenos Aires: Fly to El Calafate (3-3.5 hours), then bus or rental car to El Chaltén (3-4 hours). No direct flights to El Chaltén; it's reached only by ground transport.

🇨🇱 Desde Santiago: Fly to El Calafate, then proceed overland to El Chaltén. This requires 2-3 days of travel minimum.

🚌 Transporte local: El Chaltén is a hiking village with no airport. All arrival is by car or bus. The village itself is entirely walkable and car-free in the center.

💡 Use the daily shuttle bus (3.5-4 hours) between El Calafate and El Chaltén if you don't want to rent a car; it's affordable and scenic.

🐋 Península Valdés

✈️ Aeropuerto: Puerto Madryn Airport (PMC) or Rawson Airport (RWS)

🇦🇷 Desde Buenos Aires: Flights to Puerto Madryn (2.5 hours) on Aerolíneas Argentinas or LATAM. Buses depart Buenos Aires (16-18 hours) daily to Puerto Madryn.

🇨🇱 Desde Santiago: Limited direct flights; most connections route through Buenos Aires. Bus options available but require 24+ hours.

🚌 Transporte local: Car rental strongly recommended for exploring Península Valdés wildlife sites and coastal drives. Puerto Madryn town center is walkable.

💡 Visit September through December for optimal whale watching; book wildlife tours in advance as spots fill during peak season.

🏕️ Torres del Paine

✈️ Aeropuerto: Punta Arenas International Airport (PUQ)

🇦🇷 Desde Buenos Aires: Fly to Punta Arenas (5-6 hours with connection through Santiago). No direct flights available. This is a lengthy journey best done in stages.

🇨🇱 Desde Santiago: Fly to Punta Arenas (3.5-4 hours), then continue by bus or car to Torres del Paine (5 hours). Many visitors break this into 2-3 days.

🚌 Transporte local: Car rental essential for exploring the park. Punta Arenas offers shuttle services to Torres del Paine. No public transport within the park itself.

💡 Book accommodation inside or near Torres del Paine well in advance and arrange transport from Punta Arenas before arrival, as options are limited.

¿Cómo moverse?

✈️ Airplane

  • ✓ Fastest way to reach distant Patagonian destinations; a 3-hour flight saves 20+ hours compared to buses
  • ✓ Essential for reaching Ushuaia and southern destinations; the only practical option for many travelers
  • ✓ Multiple daily options during peak season reduce scheduling constraints
  • ✗ Most expensive transport option; flights cost 3-5 times more than bus travel
  • ✗ Subject to weather delays and cancellations, especially in Patagonia where winds can close airports

💡 Book domestic flights with Aerolíneas Argentinas or LATAM at least 2-3 weeks in advance during December-February and July for best prices.

🚌 Bus

  • ✓ Most economical transport option; often 70-80% cheaper than flights
  • ✓ Scenic routes allow you to experience Patagonian landscapes gradually
  • ✓ Overnight buses maximize travel efficiency and reduce accommodation costs
  • ✗ Extremely long journey times; distances of 20-28+ hours make buses practical only for shorter routes
  • ✗ Limited comfort on extended routes; overnight travel to distant destinations can be exhausting

💡 Use buses for shorter hops like El Calafate to El Chaltén (3.5 hours) or Puerto Madryn to Bariloche, but fly for distances over 1,000 km.

🚗 Car Rental

  • ✓ Complete freedom to explore remote areas, hidden viewpoints, and attractions on your own schedule
  • ✓ Cost-effective for groups and multi-day trips; rental fees spread across passengers
  • ✓ Access to wildlife viewing sites and scenic routes unreachable by public transport
  • ✗ Requires international driving permit and experience with varied road conditions; some routes are gravel or unpaved
  • ✗ Fuel costs and rental fees accumulate quickly; responsible for any damage or incidents

💡 Rent from major companies at airports for better insurance coverage; always purchase additional liability and theft protection in Patagonia.
