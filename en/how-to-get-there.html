How to get to Patagonia

Distances are huge: choosing how to travel wisely saves you days.

Last updated: November 2026

Most travelers reach Patagonia by plane and combine flights with buses or rental cars between destinations. Some places are hundreds of kilometers apart, so it pays to plan a few bases and calculate transfers carefully.

By destination

🏔️ Bariloche

✈️ Airport: Bariloche International Airport (BRC)

🇦🇷 From Buenos Aires: Direct flights of about 2 hours. By bus, the trip takes about a full day.

🇨🇱 From Santiago: Overland from Osorno or Puerto Montt via the Cardenal Samoré Pass.

🚌 Local transport: City buses, tours and rental cars.

💡 In January and during ski season, book flights well in advance.

🧊 El Calafate

✈️ Airport: El Calafate International Airport (FTE)

🇦🇷 From Buenos Aires: Direct flights of about 3 hours.

🇨🇱 From Santiago: Overland from Puerto Natales, crossing the border.

🚌 Local transport: Buses, tours and rental cars. The airport is about 20 km from town.

💡 It's the base for Perito Moreno and the gateway to El Chaltén.

🌍 Ushuaia

✈️ Airport: Malvinas Argentinas International Airport (USH)

🇦🇷 From Buenos Aires: Direct flights of about 3.5 hours.

🇨🇱 From Santiago: Via Punta Arenas, by plane or on a long overland trip that crosses the Strait of Magellan by ferry.

🚌 Local transport: Taxis, private cars and tours.

💡 Wind can delay flights: avoid tight connections.

⛰️ El Chaltén

✈️ Airport: No airport: the nearest is El Calafate (FTE), about 220 km away

🇦🇷 From Buenos Aires: Fly to El Calafate, then take a bus of about 3 hours.

🚌 Local transport: You get around on foot: many trails start in town.

💡 In high season, book the bus from El Calafate ahead of time.

🐋 Valdés Peninsula

✈️ Airport: Trelew (REL) and Puerto Madryn (PMY) airports

🇦🇷 From Buenos Aires: Flights to Trelew of about 2 hours, then around an hour by road to Puerto Madryn.

🚌 Local transport: Tours from Puerto Madryn or your own car. Puerto Pirámides is the starting point for whale watching.

💡 Roads inside the peninsula are gravel: drive carefully.

🏕️ Torres del Paine

✈️ Airport: Punta Arenas Airport (PUQ), Chile

🇦🇷 From Buenos Aires: Fly to El Calafate and cross overland to Puerto Natales, or fly via Santiago to Punta Arenas.

🇨🇱 From Santiago: Flights to Punta Arenas, then about 3 hours by bus to Puerto Natales.

🚌 Local transport: Buses from Puerto Natales to the park.

💡 You need confirmed reservations to sleep inside the park.

Getting around

✈️ Plane

  • ✓ The fastest way to cover long distances
  • ✓ Direct flights from Buenos Aires to the main destinations
  • ✗ Wind and weather can cause delays

💡 Fly into one city and out of another to avoid backtracking.

🚌 Bus

  • ✓ Budget-friendly option
  • ✓ Good network between Patagonian towns
  • ✗ Very long rides between some destinations

💡 For short legs, such as El Calafate–El Chaltén, it's the best choice.

🚗 Rental car

  • ✓ Freedom to stop at viewpoints and remote places
  • ✓ Convenient for groups and families
  • ✗ Long distances with widely spaced gas stations

💡 If you plan to cross into Chile, ask the rental company for permission to leave the country.

🛂 Argentina-Chile border crossings

  • Cardenal Samoré Pass — Connects Bariloche and Villa La Angostura with Osorno and Puerto Montt, Chile. It may close temporarily due to snow in winter.
  • Crossings between El Calafate and Puerto Natales — Used to combine El Calafate with Torres del Paine. Buses cover the full route.
  • Integración Austral Pass (Monte Aymond) — Connects Río Gallegos with Punta Arenas and is part of the overland route to Tierra del Fuego.
  • When entering Chile — Carry valid documents and don't bring fruit, vegetables, meat or dairy: Chile's agricultural control is strict.

💡 Booking tips

  • Book early for summer — January and February see the highest demand for flights and buses.
  • Consider open-jaw routes — Arriving in one destination and leaving from another saves days of transfers.
  • Leave time between connections — Weather can delay flights and close border crossings.
  • Book buses between towns — In high season, the busiest routes fill up.
