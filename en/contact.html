About TodoPatagonia

TodoPatagonia is an independent travel guide dedicated to Argentine and Chilean Patagonia. We publish destination guides, activities, routes and practical tips in Spanish, English and Portuguese to help you plan your trip.

How we work

Our guides are based on information checked against official sources, such as national parks and tourism agencies. We don't publish exact prices or schedules because they change often: we recommend confirming them before you travel. If you find outdated information, write to us and we'll fix it.

Affiliate links

Some links on this site are affiliate links (for example, Booking.com and GetYourGuide). If you book through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This helps us keep the site running. More details in our affiliate disclosure.
